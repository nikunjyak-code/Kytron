Privacy Policy

KYTRON Last Updated: August 22, 2026 Effective From: August 22, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how Kytron collects, uses, stores, protects and otherwise processes personal information through its website, client portal, project-management systems and related services.

Kytron is operated by Nitin Kumar.

Kytron processes personal data in accordance with applicable law, including the Indian data-protection framework as and when applicable to the relevant processing.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Kytron's services are intended for individuals aged 18 years or older.

Kytron does not intentionally invite independent account registration by persons below 18.

If Kytron becomes aware of contrary use, it may take appropriate steps subject to applicable law.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on interaction, Kytron may collect:

Identity and contact information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company/organisation name
  • Username

Account information:

  • Account credentials
  • Password information stored using appropriate security measures
  • Authentication information

Project information:

  • Project registration information
  • Requirements
  • Project descriptions
  • Deadlines
  • Budgets
  • Project history
  • Change requests
  • Project communications
  • Uploaded materials
  • Approvals
  • Acceptance records

Payment information:

  • Transaction/reference IDs
  • Payment status
  • Payment dates
  • Payment amounts
  • Invoice information

Kytron does not need to store complete payment credentials such as card numbers where payment processing is performed by an external payment provider.

Technical information:

  • IP address
  • Browser information
  • Device information
  • Login timestamps
  • Session information
  • Cookies
  • Analytics information
  • Performance/error information
  • Security logs
  • Other technical information necessary to operate and protect the platform

4. HOW WE COLLECT INFORMATION

Information may be collected when users:

  • Register projects
  • Create or use accounts
  • Access the client portal
  • Submit forms
  • Communicate with Kytron
  • Upload files
  • Request support
  • Make payments
  • Accept project terms
  • Approve changes
  • Accept completed work
  • Browse the website
  • Otherwise interact with Kytron

Authorized Kytron personnel may register projects on behalf of clients where assistance is required.

5. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

Kytron may process personal information for:

  • Account management
  • Authentication
  • Security
  • Project registration and review
  • Project development
  • Progress tracking
  • Tasks and milestones
  • Change requests
  • Payments
  • Invoicing
  • Acceptance records
  • Legal/handover documents
  • Support
  • Maintenance
  • Fraud and abuse prevention
  • Security monitoring
  • Audit
  • Troubleshooting
  • Service improvement
  • Legal compliance
  • Protecting legal rights
  • Service notifications
  • Marketing where appropriate permission/consent is required
  • Other purposes permitted or required by law

6. SERVICE COMMUNICATIONS

Kytron may send communications necessary for account management, authentication, security, project updates, approvals, payment information, invoices, legal documents, support, maintenance, deadlines, acceptance and other service-related purposes.

These are distinct from promotional marketing.

7. MARKETING

Kytron may send promotional communications where applicable consent/permission has been obtained.

Users may withdraw marketing consent or unsubscribe through available mechanisms.

Withdrawal does not prevent necessary service communications.

8. COOKIES AND TRACKING

Kytron may use:

  • Essential/session cookies
  • Persistent functionality
  • Analytics
  • Performance/error monitoring
  • Necessary technical tracking

Kytron does not currently use third-party advertising or marketing trackers as part of this Policy.

Technologies may change as the platform evolves.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICE PROVIDERS

Kytron may use third-party providers for:

  • Hosting
  • Cloud infrastructure
  • Email
  • Payment processing
  • Analytics
  • Storage
  • Security
  • Error monitoring
  • Authentication
  • Communications
  • AI
  • Other technical services

Providers may change over time.

Where third parties process data for Kytron, Kytron may implement appropriate controls required by law.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

10. DATA SHARING

Kytron may disclose or permit processing of personal information with:

Service providers: Where necessary to operate Kytron or provide services.

Project parties: Where necessary to provide an agreed project/service.

Professional advisers: Including lawyers, accountants, auditors and other professional advisers where appropriate.

Authorities: Where disclosure is required or permitted by law, court order, regulator or law-enforcement authority.

Consent-authorized disclosures: Where the user has appropriately authorized the disclosure.

Other legally permitted situations: Where disclosure is otherwise required or permitted by applicable law.

Kytron does not sell personal information as a commercial product.

11. NO ADVERTISING BUSINESS MODEL

Kytron does not currently operate its website/client portal as an advertising platform.

Kytron does not currently sell personal information to advertisers.

12. CLIENT AND PROJECT DATA

Project information may contain personal and non-personal information.

Where information belongs to the client, Kytron does not claim ownership merely because it processes or stores that information.

Client-owned information may include:

  • Customer databases
  • Customer accounts
  • Orders
  • Business information
  • Private documents
  • Proprietary information
  • Product information
  • Other information belonging to the client

The applicable legal ownership of third-party materials remains with their lawful owner.

13. INTERNAL USE OF PROJECT INFORMATION

Kytron may access project information as reasonably necessary for:

  • Service delivery
  • Development
  • Troubleshooting
  • Support
  • Maintenance
  • Security
  • Audit
  • Backups
  • Legal/accounting records
  • Dispute resolution
  • Permitted service improvement

Authorized personnel, contractors and providers may access information where necessary.

14. PERSONNEL AND CONTRACTORS

Kytron may use:

  • Employees
  • Freelancers
  • Contractors
  • Developers
  • Designers
  • Consultants
  • Support personnel
  • Specialists
  • Other authorized personnel

Access should be limited to what is reasonably necessary.

15. DATA SECURITY

Kytron uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and services.

Measures may include:

  • Access controls
  • Password security
  • Restricted access
  • Security monitoring
  • Audit logs
  • Backups
  • Incident response
  • Other appropriate safeguards

No internet-connected service guarantees absolute security.

16. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Where Kytron becomes aware of a relevant security incident involving personal information, Kytron may investigate, contain and remediate it and make notifications required by applicable law.

Where legally appropriate, Kytron may notify affected users or authorities.

17. DATA RETENTION

Kytron retains personal information only as reasonably necessary for:

  • Service delivery
  • Account management
  • Project records
  • Contractual obligations
  • Legal obligations
  • Dispute resolution
  • Security
  • Audit
  • Fraud prevention
  • Protection of legitimate interests

Retention periods vary by information type and purpose.

When no longer required, Kytron may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of information subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

18. CLIENT DATA AFTER HANDOVER

After handover, Kytron may retain relevant information for legitimate operational, contractual, legal, security, dispute-resolution or other permitted purposes.

The client does not acquire ownership of Kytron's database architecture, platform code, algorithms, security infrastructure or proprietary system information merely because project information was stored there.

19. DATA ACCESS

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to personal information held about them.

Kytron may verify identity and authority.

Disclosure may be limited to protect:

  • Another person's privacy
  • Confidential information
  • Security
  • Legal rights
  • Third-party rights
  • Kytron proprietary systems

20. CORRECTION

Users may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information, subject to applicable law and verification.

21. DELETION

Users may request deletion/erasure where such a right is available under applicable law.

Deletion may not be possible where Kytron is legally required or legitimately permitted to retain information.

22. WITHDRAWAL OF CONSENT

Where processing is based on consent, users may withdraw consent through an available mechanism.

Withdrawal does not invalidate prior lawful processing and may affect services where processing is necessary.

23. PRIVACY GRIEVANCES

Users may raise privacy/data-processing complaints through Kytron's designated contact mechanism.

Current contact information is available through the official Contact page.

Where applicable law requires a specific grievance mechanism, Kytron will provide it.

24. THIRD-PARTY REQUESTS

Kytron will not disclose personal information merely because another person requests it.

Kytron may verify identity, authority, consent, legal entitlement or other applicable basis before disclosure.

25. LEGAL AND GOVERNMENT REQUESTS

Kytron may disclose information to courts, regulators, government authorities, law-enforcement agencies or competent authorities where legally required or permitted.

Where legally permitted, Kytron may notify affected users.

26. INTERNATIONAL CLIENTS AND DATA PROCESSING

Kytron may serve clients outside India.

Information may be processed through infrastructure/providers in India or other jurisdictions subject to applicable law and safeguards.

International clients remain responsible for laws applicable to their own businesses/users.

Kytron does not represent automatic compliance with every foreign jurisdiction.

27. PROJECT COMMUNICATIONS

Kytron may retain:

  • Portal messages
  • Client instructions
  • Approvals
  • Change requests
  • Relevant emails
  • Support communications
  • Project discussions

These may be used for:

  • Project administration
  • Confirming instructions
  • Dispute resolution
  • Security
  • Legal/compliance purposes
  • Project history

28. ELECTRONIC RECORDS

Kytron may retain records relating to:

  • Terms acceptance
  • Project approval
  • Change requests
  • Final acceptance
  • Payments
  • Document generation
  • Account activity
  • Other significant actions

Records may include:

  • Timestamps
  • Account identity
  • Project ID
  • Relevant version
  • Technical audit information

29. ACCOUNT SECURITY

Users are responsible for:

  • Protecting credentials
  • Avoiding credential sharing
  • Reporting suspected unauthorized access
  • Following reasonable security instructions

Kytron may secure or suspend accounts when necessary.

30. PLATFORM SECURITY AND MISUSE

Kytron may process technical information to detect and prevent:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Fraud
  • Malware
  • Abuse
  • Attacks
  • Platform interference
  • Other security threats

Where AI is integrated into a project, information submitted to an AI service may be processed under the applicable provider's terms and privacy practices.

Clients should not provide personal, confidential or proprietary information to AI services unless they have the necessary rights/permissions and processing is appropriate.

Project-specific AI privacy arrangements may apply.

32. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Kytron may update this Policy as services, technology or legal obligations change.

Where legally required, or where a change materially affects processing based on consent, Kytron will obtain renewed consent or take other required steps.

The applicable version and effective date will be identified.

33. CONTACT

Current contact information for privacy requests, grievances and other communications is available through the official Contact page of the Kytron website.

34. APPLICABLE LAW

This Privacy Policy is governed by applicable Indian law, subject to mandatory laws applicable to particular users, processing activities or jurisdictions.

35. INTERPRETATION

Nothing in this Policy limits mandatory rights or protections available under applicable law.

Where a provision conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, that requirement prevails to the extent of the conflict.
